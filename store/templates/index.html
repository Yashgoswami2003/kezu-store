About KEZU & Co.

"Some memories fade. Some scents don't."

We wanted to make perfumes that smell like the things you almost forgot — the first rain, a childhood attic, a sunset that made you stop, a person who felt like home.

KEZU & Co. started on quiet evenings, with a heart that wanted to hold onto moments a little longer.

Founded by Queeny Yalangi — Author, poet, entrepreneur and mental health advocate. KEZU was born from the same place her stories come from — a love for feelings that stay. What started as a passion for perfumes slowly became notes in a bottle.

Every KEZU fragrance is blended by hand, in small batches, with a lot of patience and a lot of love poured into little bottles, holding big feelings.

We make them the way beautiful things should be made — with natural ingredients, soft on your skin, clean on your clothes. Only the memory stays.

Composed with fine perfume oils, layered to evolve with your skin. From the first spray to hours later — it wears like a second skin.

Each bottle is designed to linger — long-lasting, with a soft trail that stays close, not loud. The kind of luxury that doesn't need to announce itself. It just stays.

KEZU & Co — Fragrances and stories, created for every version of you.
